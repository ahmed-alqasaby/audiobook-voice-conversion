## Client ask
Take a **single recorded narration** and, via AI vocal conversion, transform it
into **distinct, believable character voices** for motivational/emotional
works and conventional fiction & non-fiction titles. Each character sounds
unique but stays consistent across chapters.

## What the client needs
1. End-to-end workflow: **model selection, training/fine-tuning, post-processing** —
   one clean reference voice → multiple character voices, suitable for long-form listening.
2. Output in **audiobook formats** (44.1kHz WAV or 192kbps MP3), mastering-ready.
3. **Reusable settings/presets** for future projects (not locked to one production).

## Acceptance criteria
- **≥4 clearly differentiated voices** from the same base narration:
  2 fiction, 1 non-fiction, 1 motivational/emotional.
- Consistent pronunciation, pacing, volume within each voice.
- **Minimal artifacts** — no robotic/metallic resonance on consumer headphones.
- Prove it on **one chapter**, then expand to entire books.
